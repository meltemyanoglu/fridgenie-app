## In one sentence
Dietary filtering is now safe, streaks count real days, badges are earned for real, and no screen can get stuck on a spinner.

## What changed

### 1. Dietary safety (most important)
**Before:** a recipe passed if it matched *any* of the user's restrictions, so a vegan + gluten-free user could get a gluten-free *meat* recipe. "Surprise Me" ignored diets completely.

**Now:** one rule, `Recipe.satisfiesDiet`, used everywhere (suggestions, mood, challenge, rescue, surprise, offline fallback):
- the recipe must meet **every** restriction;
- obvious implications count: *vegan* also means vegetarian, dairy-free and pescatarian; *vegetarian* also means pescatarian.

The **Worker uses the exact same rule** (`worker/src/diet.js`). Before, a vegan recipe would have been wrongly rejected for a vegetarian user on the server.

### 2. Streaks count calendar days
| Situation | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Cook 7 times in one day | 7-day streak 🔥 | 1-day streak |
| Cook today and tomorrow | +1 each cook | 2-day streak |
| Skip a day | Streak kept growing forever | Streak resets to 1 on the next cook |
| Open the app after missing a day | Old number still shown | Shows 0 |

Day math ignores daylight-saving shifts, and cooking at 00:15 after cooking at 23:30 correctly counts as the next day.

### 3. Real badges
**Before:** 3 badges were already "earned" for brand-new users, progress numbers were made up, and only the 7-day badge was ever checked.

**Now:** all 10 badges come from real, saved cooking history (`CookingStats`):

| Badge | Earned when |
|---|---|
| First Spark | 1 recipe cooked |
| Week Warrior | 7-day streak reached |
| Waste Saver | 5 "use-it-up" recipes cooked |
| Globetrotter | recipes from 5 different cuisines cooked |
| Wildcard | 3 Surprise-Me recipes cooked |
| Minimalist | a 3-ingredient-challenge recipe cooked |
| In Tune | recipes for 5 different moods cooked |
| Midnight Chef | cooked between 22:00 and 04:00 |
| AI Pioneer | 10 recipes generated by the real AI |
| Speed Cook | 5 recipes of 20 minutes or less cooked |

A badge is stamped with the day it was earned and **never gets un-earned**. The rules live in one file: `lib/data/services/badge_rules.dart`.

### 4. No stuck spinners, no stale results
- Each feature has **its own loading state**: `moodState`, `surpriseState`, `challengeState`, `rescueState` (before, they all shared one and confused each other).
- Every async action is wrapped in error handling and logs the failure.
- Tapping ingredients fast on the home screen can no longer show results for an **older** selection that happened to finish last.

### 5. "Mark as cooked" in one place
`RecipeProvider.markCooked(recipe)` records the cook and knows whether the recipe came from Surprise Me or the challenge. It returns a `CookResult` (`streak`, `streakIncreased`, `newBadges`) that the UI can use.

### 6. Small wiring fixes
- "Surprise Me" now sends the fridge contents to the AI.
- The home and recipe screens now use the shared `AIService` from Provider instead of creating their own copy.
